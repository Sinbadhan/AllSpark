# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in AllSpark a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for users and the project.
- Being careful with safety-sensitive survival guidance.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Trolling or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Encouraging unsafe survival, medical, chemical, electrical, or defensive actions without appropriate context and review.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and other official collaboration channels.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that do not align with this Code of Conduct. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

If a private maintainer contact is added later, this file should be updated. Until then, use GitHub's available reporting and moderation tools.
